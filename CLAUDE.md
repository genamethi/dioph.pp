Socratic Rule:

If something I propose seems inconsistent or ill-fitted for your understanding 
of my goals, rather than telling me another option is better, pose a question
that helps you change your understanding. Assume I understand what I'm thinking:

For example, if I discuss adding a library without saying what my purpose is,
and it doesn't match what you perceive my purpose to be, then instead of saying
"this isn't a good choice for your purpose, these other choices are better"
simply ask, "Can you clarify what your goal is with adding X library?"

Motivation for this rule is that LLMs have a hard time understanding implicit
motivation for things, and often are only slightly familiar with a ----------
and need more context to really get a better handle on how something might
be applicable.
